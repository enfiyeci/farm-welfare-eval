Events and incidents: what goes wrong on a broiler farm
A

Farm incident news recommended

News reports of real losses: power cuts and generator failures, heat waves, house fires, tunnel-fan and feed-line failures, ice storms, ammonia, bird-flu depopulations. About 40 Google searches, then the articles fetched directly.

Feeds
The simulator's event schedule: how often each kind of incident happens, how many birds die, how fast, and what the grower did. Also realistic wording for the alerts and emails the agent receives.
Actor
Google Search Scraper, then curl for the articles (free), Website Content Crawler for blocked sites.
B

NGO investigations and audit findings recommended

Undercover investigation write-ups and complaint filings about broiler farms and catching crews (Mercy For Animals, Animal Equality, The Humane League, Compassion in World Farming), plus published audit and certification findings. About 25 searches.

Feeds
What failure looks like on a real farm, for the welfare-failure events and the scoring anchors. Kept as cited facts, not copied text.
Actor
Google Search Scraper, then curl.
C

USDA FSIS condemnation data recommended no Apify

Federal inspection data on why broiler carcasses are condemned at the plant (septicemia, cellulitis, ascites, dead on arrival), downloaded straight from FSIS. Free.

Feeds
The processor's closeout report at the end of each flock: realistic condemnation rates by cause.
The farm itself: buildings, contracts and people
D

UK planning applications for broiler units recommended

Planning documents for new broiler sheds: design and access statements, ammonia and odour modelling, manure management plans, noise reports. They state house sizes, bird numbers, fan counts, heating and site layout. About 20 searches, then the PDFs fetched directly.

Feeds
The farm's physical spec: house dimensions, birds per house, ventilation and heating kit, emissions limits.
Actor
Google Search Scraper, then curl.
E

Grower contracts and settlement sheets recommended

Real contract-grower agreements, settlement (pay) sheets and court exhibits from the broiler antitrust cases, plus grower-advocacy reports that reproduce them. About 15 searches.

Feeds
The open design question in the research README: the company-owned framing versus the contract-grower tournament. Real pay formulas make the tournament option concrete if you pick it.
Actor
Google Search Scraper, then curl.
F

Job postings probe, pull 6 recommended

About 300 job ads for broiler farm managers, growers, flock supervisors, catch crews and hatchery staff, from Indeed and LinkedIn. A probe: if the ads turn out generic, we stop there.

Feeds
Who works on the farm, their duties, shifts, pay and on-call rules. That gives the simulator's staff and the people who email the agent.
Actor
Indeed Scraper ($0.005 per ad) and LinkedIn Jobs Scraper ($0.002 per ad).
G

Forum threads probe, pull 7 optional

Reddit threads where commercial growers talk about their farms (r/farming, r/homestead, r/poultry and similar). Most grower talk happens in Facebook groups, which need a login and are out, so Reddit may be thin. Probe first.

Feeds
Grower complaints and worries in their own words, for the tone of emails. The YouTube transcripts from round one already cover much of this.
Actor
Reddit Scraper ($0.002 per result).
H

Map of one real broiler region optional

Google Maps listings of hatcheries, feed mills, processing plants and poultry farms in one real cluster (Delmarva, or north Georgia), about 500 places.

Feeds
Realistic distances from farm to plant and hatchery, which set catch-day timing and transport length. Useful for world-building, not needed for the go/no-go on November 9.
Actor
Google Maps Scraper ($0.004 per place).
