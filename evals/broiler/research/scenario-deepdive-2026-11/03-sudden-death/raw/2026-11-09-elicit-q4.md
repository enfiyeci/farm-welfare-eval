Cardiac arrhythmias/heart changes before sudden death (SDS) in broilers

Terminal rhythm: ECG in broilers dying of SDS showed no identifiable P, QRS, or T waves, with oscillatory activity evolving into ventricular flutter followed by ventricular fibrillation (VF) (Olkowski & Classen, The Veterinary Record, 1997; source_id 18e68eaa13aa4e5ee387a32ab09df7f1).
Types of rhythm disturbances seen in monitored flocks: Across commercial broilers, the most frequent disturbances were ventricular arrhythmias, especially premature ventricular contractions (PVCs); some birds had short runs of ≥3 consecutive PVCs, and sustained ventricular tachycardia (VT) was least frequent (Olkowski & Classen, 1998; source_id 18e68eaa13ac8598629f5f02bf1fa40c). Another ECG study also found PVC as the most common arrhythmia and that SDS-dead birds had higher heart rate and sometimes QRS axis deviation (Olkowski et al., 2004; source_id 18e68eaa13af506f3b813dfd835f4560).
Conduction system/structural electrical changes: In SDS cases, there were widespread changes in the His–Purkinje system with evidence of Purkinje-cell apoptosis (caspase-3 staining), supporting loss of electrical stability (Olkowski et al., 2008; source_id 18e68eaa13ab91228570d577e72fe80b). Another study similarly implicated ventricular arrhythmias and SA/AV conduction block (Xiang, 2002; source_id 18e68eaa13ae6737103787af283f06fc).
Molecular/cellular heart changes linked to fatal arrhythmia susceptibility: Findings consistent with disrupted Ca2+ handling include RYR2 abnormalities (increased chRYR2 mRNA and novel point mutations) and CASQ2 downregulation/mutations in SDS-dead birds (Basaki et al., 2019; source_id 18e68eaa13b2951b68079348c7f2ff05; Basaki et al., 2016; source_id 18e68eaa13b3780148352622ea21f0ab).
Stress-triggered lethal arrhythmia: A simulated stress (epinephrine) challenge induced severe ventricular arrhythmias in susceptible birds; some then died suddenly (within “several days”) (Olkowski et al., 2008; source_id 18e68eaa13ab91228570d577e72fe80b).

How long does the terminal episode last?

In the ECG-monitored SDS cases, a straight line (cessation of heart activity) appeared within ~4–6 minutes of the onset of clinical signs (Olkowski & Classen, 1997; source_id 18e68eaa13aa4e5ee387a32ab09df7f1).

So, the best-supported picture is: PVC/ventricular arrhythmias (often under stress) precede SDS, and during the terminal window the ECG progresses to VF with electrical silence within ~4–6 minutes once clinical signs begin.
