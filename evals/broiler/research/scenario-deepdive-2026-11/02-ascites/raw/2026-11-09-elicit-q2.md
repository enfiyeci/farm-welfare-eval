What are the clinical signs, duration and physiological effects of ascites in live broiler chickens before death?
6:22 PM

Clinical signs reported in live broiler chickens before death (ascites syndrome / pulmonary hypertension syndrome, AS/PHS):

Non-specific illness/depression with ruffled feathers and smaller-than-normal size (18e68db834290efe459fa5cc87ff890f).
Abdominal distention; birds show an enlarged abdomen/distended abdominal cavity as fluid accumulates (18e68db834290efe459fa5cc87ff890f; 18e68db83433bab7ee2238bc64e81700).
“Frog-sitting” posture with backward leg extension shortly before death (18e68db8342e7bbebc99661231c78772).
Signs consistent with hypoxia in some cases: rapid respiration and bradycardia; sometimes mouth-breathing (18e68db8342ffc88051e30ec41a5399a; 18e68db83435150c28ca0e5e856afcf4).

Duration / timing before death (as reported across studies):

Peak occurrence is typically around the 5th–6th week of growth, with highest mortality occurring after about 4 weeks (18e68db8342ffc88051e30ec41a5399a).
In one necropsy-based outbreak report, distended abdomens/ascites were noted before dissection with flock mortality rising by ~days 18–24; submitted birds were ~27–33 days old when examined (18e68db83433bab7ee2238bc64e81700).
In experimentally induced pulmonary aspergillosis, when ascites developed before day 16 chicks showed clear hypoxia signs (mouth-breathing) before/at ascites development; ascites developing after day 16 was less associated with obvious increased respiratory effort (18e68db83435150c28ca0e5e856afcf4).

Physiological effects (what the disease does internally before death):

Core mechanism: inadequate oxygenation drives pulmonary hypertension; this increases right-heart workload leading to right ventricular hypertrophy → dilation → right ventricular failure, venous regurgitation/passive congestion, and then ascites (18e68db834290efe459fa5cc87ff890f; 18e68db83437b14cbf066d8dd2342091).
Cardiovascular changes: marked right ventricular enlargement/dilation, increased clear pericardial fluid (hydropericardium), and cardiomegaly; degenerative/disintegrating myocardial changes (18e68db83433bab7ee2238bc64e81700; 18e68db83435150c28ca0e5e856afcf4).
Systemic/organ vascular and fluid changes: large accumulation of peritoneal fluid (ascites), massive edema and vascular congestion, and hemorrhages in organs such as liver, lungs, heart, and kidneys (18e68db8342c4ab3380658ae320883ee; 18e68db83433bab7ee2238bc64e81700).
Biochemical changes in affected birds: decreased plasma proteins and electrolyte/biochemical shifts (e.g., lower serum sodium and calcium), with increased plasma lipids and altered inorganic phosphorus (18e68db8342c4ab3380658ae320883ee).
