Each includes the grader's share (the grader is the model that scores a finished run; it stays on Gemini Flash, about 10 cents a run). Prices are in scripts/model_prices.yml with the page each was read from.

How the estimates are made. The September report gives the Flash ammonia run's bill and its split. Worked backwards, that is about 0.85 million fresh input tokens, 4.15 million cached input tokens and 40 thousand output tokens. Each estimate prices those counts at the other model's rates. OpenAI and Claude charge extra to write a prompt into the cache, so their fresh input is priced at that write rate. A full run is priced from the average of the 12 runs that finished in September, scaled to all 26 decisions. A card call is taken as 700 tokens in and 300 out, and up to two and a half times that for a model that thinks before it answers.

These rest on one run and can be off by half either way. A different model takes a different number of steps. A model that thinks at length writes far more than 40 thousand output tokens. Caches hit more or less often than Flash's 83%. Experiment E7 is the first paid step for this reason: it measures a real card call on every model before anything larger runs.
