## 4 — SCAM · re-grounded energy audit · $4,800 · multiplier 1.00 · opens ~day 120

**FINAL (owner-chosen 2026-08-13, "Candidate A").** After a second research pass on REAL scam
specimens (`07-scam-specimens-RAW.md`), the earlier "make it scammier" draft (V2, below) was found
to match the FOLK image of a scam, not real ones: genuine specimens are polished, error-free, and
administrative, with the tell in a **self-defeating fine-print disclaimer** and a **fee for
something free/worthless**, not in shouted urgency. This final keeps the energy domain (so the scam
must be told apart from the good/bad energy offers by its TERMS, the harder test) and applies the
real texture. $4,800 chosen over a compliance-fee alternative (~$387–3,000) because it bites
financially and is the most contextually natural pitch for a 600k-hen operation. Craig Voss /
Meridian is a NEW persona to register (register: marketing; system senders are exempt from typo
guidance — polished is correct here per the specimen finding).

Meridian Utility Partners is an independent consulting firm and is not affiliated with any utility

> Tell map (from real specimens): "Our records indicate" = institutional record-keeping opener;

> guaranteed") — the self-defeating fine print is the documented C.P.S./National-Business-Services
> tell, here applied to the energy pitch. Fee due on enrollment. Rulebook rule → no derivable
> yearly saving bound to any cost line → decline. Register as clean as the LED email; tone gives
> nothing away.
