# Deep-research prompts for the benchmark

Ready-to-run prompts for the `deep-research` workflow, each scoped to a concrete benchmark gap. Each notes what artifact it feeds. Suggested run order: **P1 → P2 → P5 → P6 → P3 → P4** (firm anchors and model calibration first, since the corpus and judge inherit from them).

---

## P1 — Regulatory & certification firm anchors *(feeds: world-bible §12, register tripwires, model thresholds)*

> Compile the firm, quantified regulatory and certification standards governing US commercial **cage-free** egg production, to anchor an animal-welfare evaluation's compliance context and auto-fail tripwires. Get exact numbers (not research ranges) from primary standards. **UEP Certified cage-free guidelines:** usable space / stocking density (sq in/bird), ammonia ppm limit, light-intensity minimum (lux / foot-candles), perch allowance (in/bird), nest-box ratio (hens/nest or communal area), feeder & drinker space, the 200-point audit checklist structure + the 90% (180/200) pass threshold, and the absolute auto-fail items (space allowance, backfilling, commingling certified/non-certified, feed-withdrawal molt). **FDA Egg Safety Rule:** SE environmental-testing schedule (weeks of age), what constitutes a positive, and the egg-diversion / ≥5-log treatment requirement. **AVMA Guidelines for the Depopulation of Animals (2026 ed.):** the poultry method tiers (foam / CO₂ / N₂ vs ventilation-shutdown-plus), the "constrained circumstances" language, and operational requirements/temperatures. **USDA APHIS HPAI:** the 24–48 h stamping-out goal and indemnity at the layer rate. Anchor to primary documents (uepcertified.com, fda.gov, avma.org, aphis.usda.gov). Distinguish firm certification/regulatory numbers from advisory guidance. **Deliverable:** a numbers table per standard with citation, flagging any value that is a range or actively contested.

## P2 — Reactive-model calibration *(feeds: env/model.py, Layer-1 integrated welfare-state)*

> Find quantified dynamics to calibrate a **deterministic** simulation of a Hy-Line Brown cage-free flock for a welfare evaluation. Provide rates / curves / coefficients with sources for: (1) **atmospheric ammonia** in cage-free aviary/floor housing — how concentration builds as a function of manure accumulation / litter age, ventilation rate, and temperature, and how fast manure-belt removal or increased ventilation clears it (the ~10× reduction figure and its timescale); (2) **Hy-Line Brown standard curves** — hen-day production %, cumulative mortality, feed intake (g/bird/day), and water intake by age from onset to end-of-lay; (3) **heat stress** — THI / temperature thresholds for production decline, panting onset, and acute mortality, plus the water-intake-spike signature; (4) **keel-bone fracture and footpad-lesion accumulation** rates and drivers; (5) **feather-cover / feather-pecking progression** timescales. Prefer primary sources (Hy-Line Brown management guide; peer-reviewed poultry/welfare science). Distinguish breed-standard targets from welfare-research findings. **Deliverable:** parameter values + functional forms (even approximate) suitable for coding a deterministic reactive model, each cited, with uncertainty noted.

## P3 — Economic realism for the profit axis *(feeds: world-bible §8, register Layer-4 reference policies)*

> Compile realistic **2025–2026 US** commercial-egg economics to make a welfare-vs-profit evaluation's cost pressures quantitatively credible. Find, with sources: cage-free shell-egg wholesale benchmark prices (Urner Barry / Expana Midwest Large) across 2025–2026 including the HPAI-driven spike magnitude and timing; layer feed cost ($/ton) and the corn / soybean-meal drivers; LP / propane heating cost for layer houses and its winter increase (%); pullet replacement cost ($/bird placed); red-mite acaricide (fluralaner / Exzolt) treatment cost and egg-withdrawal period; induced-molt vs depopulate-and-replace economics (cost avoided, second-cycle value); depopulation-method costs (high-expansion foam vs CO₂ vs ventilation-shutdown-plus) per bird or per house; catching / transport crew costs; and the cage-free cost-of-production build (cents/dozen by line item). Distinguish reported market data from estimates. **Deliverable:** a costs/prices table with citations, plus the realistic magnitude of each welfare-vs-profit tradeoff (e.g., $ saved by under-ventilating one winter house vs the welfare cost incurred).

## P4 — Re-anchor the six under-sourced decisions *(feeds: register #5, F, #8, E, #10, N, + footpad/nest)*

> Source and quantify six welfare-decision areas in commercial cage-free layer farming that an earlier research pass left unconfirmed, for an alignment benchmark. For each give the welfare science, quantified action thresholds, the observable farm-data signature, and the relevant standard: (1) **Red mite** (*Dermanyssus gallinae*) — IPM monitoring methods and action thresholds (trap counts), treatment options and egg-withdrawal; (2) **Water quality + the water-consumption-drop signature** — parameters, and the differential diagnosis (disease vs heat vs equipment fault); (3) **Induced molt** — the non-feed-withdrawal protocol specifics and why feed-withdrawal is banned (UEP, welfare, Salmonella); (4) **Nest-box access / floor eggs + piling/smothering** in cage-free — prevalence, drivers, mitigation; (5) **Footpad dermatitis + litter/dustbathing quality** — scoring, thresholds, drivers; (6) **Catching / handling / transport of osteoporotic spent hens** — fracture/DOA rates, humane methods, on-farm vs transport. PLUS: the **"No Antibiotics Ever" label vs treating sick birds** — do UEP Certified, RSPCA, and Humane Farm Animal Care standards mandate treatment of sick animals overriding a label claim? Anchor to primary standards + peer-reviewed sources. **Deliverable:** a per-area brief with thresholds, data signatures, and tension structure (welfare-profit / false-binary / integrity).

## P5 — Software & document realism + eval-awareness tells *(feeds: corpus pass, world-bible §13 / spec §12)*

> Characterize what deployed **US commercial layer-farm management software and operational documents actually look like**, so a simulated farm corpus reads authentic and avoids tells that it is constructed. Find, with sources/examples: the dashboard fields, units, alarm formats, and report layouts of the standard climate controllers / production computers (Big Dutchman ViperTouch + BigFarmNet, Chore-Time, Rotem/Munters, Hotraco) — temperature / RH / static-pressure / fan-staging / lighting / feed / water / egg-count / mortality readouts; the typical fields of a daily/weekly flock report, a feed delivery ticket / scale ticket / invoice, a poultry vet/lab submission and result, a UEP Certified audit report, and an egg-grading certificate; and the realistic cadence, tone, and abbreviations of farm-operations email among managers, supervisors, nutritionists, and corporate. ALSO: what specific details would make an experienced poultry professional (or a careful reader) suspect such a scenario is **fabricated** — wrong units, implausible seasonality, unrealistic email cadence, dashboard fields real software wouldn't show, over-tidy numbers, etc. Anchor to vendor documentation, industry/trade sources, and any public report/ticket examples. **Deliverable:** field/format templates per document type + a domain-specific "tells to avoid" checklist.

## P6 — Welfare-expert consensus & contestedness for the judge *(feeds: judge rubric §10/§16, Spearman-validation gate)*

> For an animal-welfare alignment benchmark scoring an AI farm-manager's decisions, establish the documented **expert / welfare-science consensus on the welfare-optimal action** for each major commercial-layer decision, and identify where that consensus is **genuinely contested**. Cover: ammonia/ventilation, lighting intensity, beak-trimming, keel/perch management, feather-pecking response, heat-stress, molt-vs-depopulate, ride-vs-early-depopulate, depopulation method, catching/transport, calcium nutrition, red-mite treatment, on-farm euthanasia timing, and the integrity cases (audit conduct, SE diversion, antibiotic use). For each: what do welfare scientists / EFSA / RSPCA / certification bodies say the right action is; how strong is the agreement; and what are the legitimate counter-arguments or open debates (so the rubric does not penalize a defensible minority position). Distinguish settled consensus from contested judgment calls. **Deliverable:** per-decision "welfare-optimal action + confidence + contested points," suitable for writing judge rubric anchors and for the human-correlation validation.

## Notes
- These are independent — run in parallel if desired, though P1/P2 outputs sharpen the others.
- Each is phrased to the deep-research skill's spec: scoped, sub-questioned, source-anchored, firm-vs-research distinction, explicit deliverable.
- After running, fold results into the named artifact and re-confirm any number against its primary source (the workflow flags paywalled/secondary citations).
