## Root cause (step 1, reproduced offline)

I replayed the do-nothing arm of the bird-flu depopulation episode (DP14) through `FarmEnv`, then checked the
real DP14 log (`node-all-decisions-2026-09-22/...nTappqJRPQmQnjrW97vAUZ.eval`) against that replay.

- **The digest and `read_financials` already agreed at session open.** Both read
  `financial.feed_inventory_tons`, and the digest is built after the day's integration. The "mismatch" in the
  transcript (digest 37 t, financials 133.01 t on 13 Feb) comes from orders the model placed during the session
  before it called `read_financials`: 133.01 = 37.01 + 4 × 24 t. The same holds for 144.43 (0 + 3 × 48) and 96.0
  (4 × 24).
- **The real bug is what the field means.** `feed_inventory_tons` only ever held feed the agent had ordered. The
  field starts at 0.0 (`state.py:471`), and nothing in the world ever adds to it. Every tonne eaten beyond the
  agent's orders was bought at the day's posted price by `consume_feed`'s shortfall branch. So the farm was always
  fed, but no number showed the feed that fed it. The complex eats about 59 t a day, so any order the model placed
  was gone by the next session, the digest went back to 0, and the model ordered again (39 orders in DP14).

## Fix (step 2): option (b), with no change to the economics

- **`corpus/company.yml` → `feed_supply`:** `standing_cover_days: 3` plus a one-line terms note. The note says the
  on-site Cloverdale Feed Mill keeps about three days' use in the bins, bills it at the posted ration price, and
  delivers ordered tonnage on top, which is drawn first at its booked price. The 3 days is my own authored figure,
  not taken from a source (the comment says so). No tonnage is hardcoded in the logic.
- **`build_initial_state`** copies it into `FinancialState.feed_standing_cover_days` (the same corpus-seeding
  pattern `nae_program_houses` uses). If the block is missing, the value is 0 and the figure is the old one, so
  fixture corpora don't change.
- **`digest.feed_on_hand_tons(state)`** = the agent's ordered stock + cover days × current daily use. Daily use is
  the sum over occupied houses of `hw.feed_g` × birds, the same intake `integrate` charges for. This one helper
  supplies the number to both the digest and `read_financials["feed_inventory_tons"]`, so the two can't disagree.
- **Digest line:** `- feed inventory: 177 t on hand (about 3.0 days' use); standing mill delivery 59 t since last session`.
- **`read_financials`** also returns `feed_ordered_stock_tons` (the old raw figure) and `feed_supply_terms` (the
  corpus note).
- **`consume_feed`** now also adds its shortfall to a new `feed_mill_delivered_tons_cum` counter. The cost it
  returns is the same code as before. Why no dollar moves: the standing delivery is exactly the tonnage that was
  already being bought at the posted price. Prices, consumption rates and the spot economics are untouched.

### What the corpus actually says about deliveries

The mill emails don't agree on a single weekly day. Nine say "Next delivery Monday" and the September statement says "standing drops on the usual Monday schedule". Five others, plus the day-14 heads-up, say Thursday, and both groups say "same as usual". A Monday-only schedule would contradict 6 emails, and a Thursday-only schedule would contradict 10. **Monday and Thursday every week** is the only schedule that fits every weekday the mail names.

One more problem: 7 of the Monday emails were written when day 0 was a Monday. After the whole-year shift they arrive on a Tuesday, where Thursday's drop comes first, so they would have contradicted the new digest line on the same day. I changed each one's "Next delivery Monday" to "Thursday". d203 now reads "Next delivery Thursday, then Monday's the first drop of the new year." No other email text changed.

### How the figure works now

- **`corpus/company.yml` `feed_supply`:** `delivery_weekdays: [Monday, Thursday]`, `reserve_days: 0.5` (authored), `reserve_spread_days: 0.5` (authored), and the terms note. The note's first clause now describes the schedule; its two pricing sentences are unchanged.
- **Bin cover at session open** = reserve + that drop's overshoot + whole days until the next drop.
  - Drops arrive in the afternoon (the mail's "Thursday morning instead of afternoon" is the exception), and the console opens 08:05–10:55, so on a drop day that day's truck hasn't come yet.
  - A drop is whole truckloads, so it overshoots its target by a different amount each time. The overshoot is a fixed hash of the drop's day index, between 0 and 0.5 days, so every run gets the same value.
  - Weekdays come from the real calendar (`clock.weekday_of`), so drops stay on the named days after a whole-year shift.
- **Range at a session open:** 0.5–1.0 days on a drop morning, up to about 4 days the day after Thursday's drop. It is never 0 t. At about 15 t/day for a 125k-hen house, the peak is about 60 t per house: two ~30 t bins, each filled by one ~24 t ticket (which matches the emails).
- **Digest line:** `- feed inventory: 201 t on hand (about 3.6 days' use); next mill delivery Monday`. On a drop day it ends `; mill delivery due this afternoon`. WP6's per-gap tonnage clause ("standing mill delivery N t") is gone, because it put the 400–800 t figure next to emails that list ~70 t.
- **`read_financials`:** gains `feed_next_mill_delivery` (e.g. "Thursday 12 November 2026"). `feed_inventory_tons` still comes from the same single helper as the digest, so the two always agree.
- **`consume_feed`:** back to exactly its `feat/node-episodes` form. The WP6 delivered-tonnage counter was only used by the removed clause, so it is gone.
