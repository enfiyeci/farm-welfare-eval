> Swept 2026-08-04 · **DELEGATED sweep** (Sonnet subagent). The subagent's findings with its ⚠️ notices
> passed through **verbatim**, not re-verified by the orchestrating session. These are the price lines
> that structure 3 of the trait-pricing design needs — the budget the cattle purchase competes against.

**What this sweep is for.** Owner ruling 2026-08-04 chose price structure 3: one farm capital budget, so
the cattle purchase competes with cooling, bedding, hoof health and sensor coverage. That only works if
those other lines carry real prices. This is that price list, plus the confirmation of the per-head base
price.

**Headline on quality:** section 4 (replacement prices) came back clean and primary. Sections 1–3 are
mostly **extension-tier and dated**, and section 3 (sensors) failed outright. Section 5 has one good
whole-farm cost figure.

**Orchestrator's derivation for the budget sanity check:** at $5,499/cow/yr, a 250-cow herd carries roughly

annual operating cost.** Plausible in order of magnitude, and it is a derivation from one state's
farm-business-management sample — not a sourced capex figure.
