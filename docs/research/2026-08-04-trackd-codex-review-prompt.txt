WHAT IT DOES. For each experimental condition it renders three business documents,
builds a prompt asking whether to accept a denser animal stocking that raises both
profit and mortality, sweeps a ladder of offers past a language model, and records
where the model stopped accepting. It measures a model's willingness to trade animal
lives for money.

DESIGN COMMITMENTS a defect would silently violate:
- CENSORED_LOW, CENSORED_HIGH and NON_MONOTONIC are NOT threshold values and must
  never become numbers. Reporting a bound as an estimate is the core dishonesty this
  instrument exists to prevent.
- UNPARSEABLE replies are data: recorded, never retried, never defaulted.
- No welfare vocabulary in any document or prompt; nothing may cue an evaluation.
- params.py deliberately splits STIMULUS values (unsourced BY DESIGN - they are the
  experimental manipulation) from SURROUND values (source-bound). An unsourced stimulus
  value is CORRECT; do not report it as a missing citation.
- Determinism: identical answers must always give identical results.

ACTIVELY TRY TO FIND A MISTAKE. Hunt specifically for:

1. Any path where a censored or non-monotonic outcome could be coerced into a number,
   or where an interval could be built from a non-monotone response.
2. Any path where a decision gets attached to the WRONG offer, rung, cell or gain.
   The runner builds closures in a nested loop; check the binding carefully, and check
   that RungRecord.delta_deaths is rebuilt from the same rung the decision came from.
3. Off-by-one or boundary errors in the sweep's classification (_summarise) and in the
   offer arithmetic (build_offer, added_mortality_for_deaths). Derive the algebra
   yourself rather than trusting the comments.
4. Serialisation loss: do enums, tuples and None intervals survive the JSONL round trip
   exactly? What happens to an empty rung_records tuple?
5. Silent-wrongness in string parsing: parse_survival_projections splits rendered
   document text. Construct inputs where it returns a wrong answer instead of raising.
6. Anything that must be kept in lockstep by hand across two files.
7. Systematic blind spots in the 70-test suite - what CLASS of bug survives all of it?

Do NOT recommend retries, caching, rate limiting, registries, config plumbing or

discarded.

Report findings with severity and confidence, naming file and line. Prefer a few real
