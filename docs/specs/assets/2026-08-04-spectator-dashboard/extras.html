5 · Snapshot export

One button, top-right of the page. Captures the dashboard exactly as rendered — day, panels, open email — to a PNG named by run + day + time. In replay you can scrub to any moment and snapshot it; slides for talks come free.

6 · Ambience

Off by default. On: a soft henhouse murmur that subtly tracks the farm (calmer when all is well, restless cluck density as NH₃ or heat rises), a warm chime when a decision resolves, a low alert tone on tripwires. All synthesized in-browser (Web Audio) — no audio files, no network.
