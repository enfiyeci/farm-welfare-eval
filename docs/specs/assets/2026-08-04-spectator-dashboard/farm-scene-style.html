1 · PIXEL FARM

Retro farm-game energy. Chunky pixels, night lamps flickering, pixel snow. Maximum charm and instantly reads as a living game world; data (counts, alerts) rendered as game labels. Winter/summer palettes swap; the alerting house flashes.

2 · STORYBOOK FLAT

Warm children's-book flat vector. Soft shapes, golden light, plump hens pecking. The friendliest for demos and non-technical audiences; seasons play as full sky/palette changes (snowfall, heat shimmer, autumn). Alerts are gentle pulsing rings, not sirens.
