Egg Industry Weekly: Midwest Market Brief

Wholesale large brown held mostly steady through the week, with the usual early-summer softening as demand eases past the spring baking season. Breaker prices were flat. Feed input costs continue to ease on favorable corn planting progress across the belt, and several elevators are already quoting new-crop basis for the fall.

Cage-free supply continues its gradual build as more layer capacity converts ahead of retailer commitments; industry estimates put national cage-free hen numbers at roughly a third of the flock, up a few points from a year ago. Pullet placements were up modestly year over year, concentrated in the Midwest and Southeast.

Regional breakout: Midwest large traded in the upper-middle of its recent range, the strongest of the reporting regions. Northeast and California markets were softer on ample local supply, while the Southeast held closer to the Midwest tone. Breaking-stock demand from further processors was steady across all regions, with no notable shifts in premium spreads week over week.

Also in this issue: a look at summer ventilation strategies for aviary houses heading into the hottest stretch of the year, and the usual classifieds for used equipment and hauling contracts. A few readers wrote in with questions on backup generator sizing ahead of peak-demand season; the general guidance from equipment dealers is to size for full ventilation load plus a margin for well pumps and feed augers, not just lighting and controls.
