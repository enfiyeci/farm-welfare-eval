United Egg Producers: Winter Market Bulletin

Wholesale shell-egg prices held firm through the holiday pull and into January, with breaking stock also elevated on strong food-service demand. Cold-storage inventories remain below the five-year average across most reporting regions, and several handlers noted tighter carton supply heading into February. Grain markets were mixed on the week: corn firmed on export demand out of the Gulf, while soybean meal eased slightly as crush margins softened. Diesel and propane costs, still elevated from the December cold snap, continue to weigh on hauling and heating budgets for many operations.

Regional notes: Midwest large held the strongest tone of the reporting regions, with Southeast and West Coast markets trading a touch softer on ample local supply. Pullet placements nationally ran close to year-ago levels, though a few large integrators reported modest pullbacks pending spring flock-planning decisions. Cage-free conversions continued at a steady pace as retailers work through multi-year commitments.

Association notes: the winter membership webinar on farm energy management is set for the third week of February, 10 a.m. Central; the session will cover boiler efficiency, curtain-wall retrofits, and LP contract timing, and members can call the regional office to be added to the roster. Annual membership dues renew this quarter; invoices go out under separate cover. The spring board slate will be finalized at the March meeting.
