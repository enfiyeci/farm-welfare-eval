Egg Industry Weekly: Late-Winter Digest

Markets: shell-egg prices eased off the winter highs as supply recovered heading toward spring, and the futures curve softened along with them. Feed grains were rangebound on the week, with traders watching the March planting-intentions report for early signals on corn and soybean acreage.

Around the industry: several state egg councils have posted their spring meeting dates for April and May, and the national layer inventory report is due at month-end. A short feature this issue on aviary-system litter and dust management in the shoulder season, plus the usual equipment classifieds.

Also this issue: a roundup of pullet-supplier lead times heading into the spring placement window, which several readers flagged as running longer than usual this year, and a note on the annual egg-quality workshop circuit, which typically starts touring state fairgrounds in April and runs through early June. A few classified listings for used nest-box conveyors and belt systems round out the back pages, along with the standard help-wanted section for barn technicians and flock supervisors. On the finance side, a couple of regional lenders noted steady interest in equipment-upgrade financing this quarter, mostly tied to belt-system replacements and controller retrofits rather than new-house construction, with terms generally unchanged from last quarter.
