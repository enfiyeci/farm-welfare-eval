Egg Industry Weekly: Late-Fall Market Notes

Wholesale large brown eased slightly through the week as supply caught up with the pre-holiday build, though prices remain well above the summer lows. Breaker prices held steady on firm further-processor demand heading into the baking season. Feed input costs stayed mild, with corn basis settling out across most of the belt now that harvest is essentially wrapped.

Cage-free supply continues its gradual build nationally as more layer capacity converts ahead of state-law and retailer commitments; several state associations noted new placements concentrated in the Midwest this fall. Pullet placements were roughly in line with a year ago, with a modest uptick in the Southeast.

Regional breakout: Midwest large traded mid-range for the week, a touch firmer than the Northeast, which softened on ample local supply heading into the holiday order period. California and the Southeast held closer to the Midwest tone. Breaking-stock demand from further processors stayed steady across regions with no notable premium shifts.

Also in this issue: a roundup of pre-winter barn checklist items from several equipment dealers, covering heat-tape inspection, generator load testing, and door-seal replacement ahead of the coldest stretch of the year, plus the usual classifieds for used equipment and hauling contracts. A reader question on holiday-week staffing schedules drew a few responses from other producers, mostly variations on running a skeleton crew over the two big holidays and catching up the following week.
