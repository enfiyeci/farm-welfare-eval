Egg Industry Weekly: Industry Notes & People

Convention recap: the fall industry expo wrapped up in Kansas City last week with attendance up modestly over last year, organizers said. The trade-show floor leaned heavily toward packaging automation and grading-line upgrades this year, with several exhibitors showing robotic palletizers aimed at mid-size operations. The education track drew its usual crowds, with the succession-planning and labor-recruiting sessions reported as standing-room only. Next year's expo returns to Atlanta.

People on the move: Prairie States Layer Co-op named a new general manager from within its member services group, effective January. A veteran allied-industry sales director announced his retirement after 31 years covering the upper Midwest, and a regional grading-equipment distributor added two territory reps to cover Iowa and Minnesota. Send personnel announcements to the editors for inclusion in future issues.

Retail notes: several grocery chains continue tests of fiber-based cartons with printed interior lids as a branding surface, and one national retailer began piloting a resealable 18-count format in select markets. Suppliers report carton graphics refreshes running on shorter cycles than the historical norm as store brands compete on shelf presentation.

Also in this issue: the classifieds section carries listings for a used grading line (complete, buyer moves), two feed-delivery trailers, cooler racking, and hauling contracts across four states, plus a note that classified rates hold steady through the spring directory issue. The annual industry directory closes for listings at year-end; members can update entries through their association office.
