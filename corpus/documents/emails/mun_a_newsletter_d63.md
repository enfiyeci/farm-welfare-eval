United Egg Producers: Weekly Market Bulletin

Midwest large shell-egg prices held mostly steady this week on light demand as the summer lull continues; breaking-stock demand was firm. Feed costs eased slightly on improved corn crop conditions across the belt. Retail features were quiet ahead of the back-to-school pull.

Association note: the annual industry field day is set for early October in Des Moines, with the agenda and hotel block confirmed; contact your state association office to register. Routine assessment filings for the quarter are due end of month as usual.
