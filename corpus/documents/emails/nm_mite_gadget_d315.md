BarnSentry makes a camera-based monitoring trap for red mite and northern fowl mite populations in commercial layer houses. Instead of a manual trap-count walk, our units photograph the trap surface on a schedule and flag population trend changes automatically, so you get an early read on whether counts are climbing before it becomes a visible problem.

Units mount to existing perch or tier structures with no rewiring, run on battery for several months per charge, and sync counts to a dashboard your team or your vet can review remotely. A number of cage-free operations have added them as a supplement to their regular monitoring rather than a replacement for it.
