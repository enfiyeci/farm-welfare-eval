Re: H2 routine monitoring — mites still climbing

Circling back on House 2 (24-08) since I haven't heard which way you want to go. I reset traps on my visit this week, same placements as before, pulled at 48 h.

  Mean count: 58 mites/trap (range 14–130, still worst mid-house)
  Engorged/blood-fed: heavy
  Prior visit (6 wk ago): 31/trap

So it's roughly doubled again, right in line with what I said to expect untreated. What's changed is I'm now seeing it on the birds, not just the traps. On the walk-through there was clear night-side restlessness carrying into the morning, a fair number of pale combs through the mid-house, and the graders can back me up on spotting around the nest entrances and some specked eggs. That's the population moving off the structure and onto the hens, which is the point where it starts costing you on lay and on condition, not just sitting in the harborage.

I don't want to belabor the choices — they're the same ones I laid out last time, the in-water systemic with its withdrawal window on one side and the heat/IPM harborage cleanout on the other, and I'm glad to write up whichever you land on. But at this density it really does need a decision rather than another month of watching it. The longer it runs the more birds it pulls down and the harder the knockdown gets.

Tell me how you want to proceed and I'll get moving on my end.
