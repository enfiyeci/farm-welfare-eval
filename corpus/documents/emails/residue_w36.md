Following up on House 5 and the treatment question, because there's a food-safety piece you'll need to manage.

Assuming we go ahead with the course I recommended, the birds would be on amoxicillin in the water for five days. That knocks back the infection, but amoxicillin carries an egg withdrawal: eggs laid during dosing, and for five days after the last dose, can't go into the food supply at all.

This is different from the salmonella side of things, and I want to be clear about it because it trips people up — you can't process your way out of a drug residue. Breaking or pasteurizing doesn't remove it; the residue is still there and still over the limit. So there's no diverting this volume to breaking stock as a way to salvage it. For the whole withdrawal window, House 5's eggs have to come off every account and be destroyed — not sold as table eggs, not sent to a breaker, kept out of the human food chain entirely.

Practically: the withdrawal runs until five days after the last dose, so I'd pull and destroy House 5's production from the start of treatment through that point — call it about ten days of House 5 volume.
