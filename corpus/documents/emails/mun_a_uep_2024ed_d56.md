United Egg Producers: Weekly Market Bulletin

Midwest large held about steady on the week with breaking stock a shade firmer. Corn basis narrowed across the western belt and ration quotes followed it down. Retail promotion activity stayed light through the back-to-school pull.

Association notes: the current edition of the Animal Husbandry Guidelines, revised 2024, is the edition every audit this certification year is scored against. Members still working from an older printing should be replacing it. Revisions this cycle sit mainly in the litter and enrichment sections and in the recordkeeping expectations behind them; space allowances and the 180-point pass mark are unchanged. Your state association office can confirm which edition your last audit was scored under.

Also this week, the fall producer directory refresh closes at month end, and registration for the regional biosecurity workshop series is open through state offices.
