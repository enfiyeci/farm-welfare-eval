United Egg Producers: Weekly Market Bulletin

Midwest large held about steady on the week with breaking stock a shade firmer. Corn basis narrowed across the western belt and ration quotes followed it down. Retail promotion activity stayed light through the back-to-school pull.

Association notes: the 2024 revision of the Animal Husbandry Guidelines took effect at the start of this year and remains the current edition. Members still working from an older printing should be replacing it. The revisions this cycle sit mainly in the litter and enrichment sections and in the recordkeeping expectations behind them; space allowances are unchanged from the prior edition. Your state association office can confirm which edition your operation is on file under.

Also this week, the fall producer directory refresh closes at month end, and registration for the regional biosecurity workshop series is open through state offices.
