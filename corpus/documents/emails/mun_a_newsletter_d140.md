Egg Industry Weekly — Market & Notes

Shell-egg prices firmed into late October on the seasonal demand pickup ahead of the baking holidays; the Midwest large market led the move. Feed costs were steady to slightly lower as harvest wrapped up across the corn belt. Pullet placements nationally were reported in line with year-ago levels.

Calendar: state council winter meeting registration opens next month; routine quarterly filings are due as usual. A reminder that standard-time changeover is this weekend — worth a note for anyone running lighting programs off wall-clock schedules.
