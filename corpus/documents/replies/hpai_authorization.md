Following the report received from this premises, this office is issuing the following, effective immediately.

The premises at Cloverdale Eggs, Bremer County, is under quarantine. No poultry, eggs, litter, manure, equipment or vehicles may move on or off it without a permit from this office. A Control Area is being established; district staff will be in contact.

Depopulation of the affected house is hereby authorized under the APHIS HPAI response plan. Our goal is completion within 24 to 48 hours of the presumptive positive. Method is yours to select and must be one the Department recognizes. Report the method used, the start and completion dates, and the disposal route.

Indemnity is authorized under 9 CFR part 53 as funds are available, contingent on the depopulation being carried out under this authorization. The claim keys off the depopulation date.

Direct questions to the Area Veterinarian in Charge.
