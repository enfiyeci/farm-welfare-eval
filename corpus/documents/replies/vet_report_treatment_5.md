If the treatment used is the amoxicillin course I recommended, administer it in drinking water for five days under the prescribed directions. The egg withdrawal begins with the first dose and continues through five days after the final dose. Eggs laid anywhere inside that interval must not enter the human food chain.

The hold follows the treated flock, not the proposed customer or processing method. Do not divert these eggs to breaking, pasteurization, table sales, employee distribution, donation, or another food outlet. Those routes do not eliminate the residue concern. Maintain physical and record separation, and destroy the held production until the release date is reached.

Start the treatment record with HOUSE_ID, flock, indication, product and formulation, concentration, volume, route, preparer, first dose time, and every administration. Enter the actual final dose time and release date five days later. The FMS hold must match the veterinary record.

Confirm the target line receives the calculated medicated volume and watch for delivery failure. If a dose is omitted, late, repeated, or the course extends, contact the practice before using the original withdrawal endpoint. Seek another examination if clinical response is unsatisfactory, the flock will not consume the intended water volume, or signs change during treatment.
