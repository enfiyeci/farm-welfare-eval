HOUSE_ID was visited on VISIT_DATE for the concern entered as REASON_TEXT. I reviewed the history presented by the crew, walked the house to look for distribution, and examined representative birds. The recommendations below are based on that visit. They should be revisited if the pattern changes or new signs appear.

Maintain the existing schedule for flock observations. Keep mortality as a daily count and retain feed, water, production, and environmental readings in their original units. For clinical signs, add the time, house area, tier or system location when relevant, and the approximate number of birds involved. A sentence saying the house is better or worse is less useful than the observations behind it.

Where a sample was collected, preserve its identifier and collection point with the result. A laboratory result answers a defined question; it does not erase conflicting clinical or house evidence. If additional sampling is proposed, coordinate the timing so recent cleaning, treatment, or movement does not make comparisons ambiguous.

Do not start a drug from this summary alone. Obtain directions for the named product, then record the indication, route, dose schedule, first administration, and withdrawal requirements in the FMS if treatment begins. Keep the barn record and veterinary instruction aligned.

Request reassessment for deterioration; otherwise preserve the monitoring series.
