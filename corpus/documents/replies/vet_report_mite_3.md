Presenting concern: REASON_TEXT

Use a sequence of comparable trap reads. Keep cards at fixed lower, middle, and upper positions, including night roosts. Label location, set time, pull time, and every count. Retain zeroes and high results.

My recommendation is to graph the like-for-like counts in time order. A rise repeated across consecutive reads and several positions supports intervention before the burden becomes broadly established. A single hot card warrants checking its immediate area and confirming the next read. Stable or declining counts support continued surveillance, provided card positions and exposure intervals have not changed.

If that trend reaches the treatment threshold, I recommend Exzolt in drinking water: two administrations seven days apart, zero-day egg withdrawal, extralabel for red mite in the US, on prescription. Calculate the medicated volume for the birds served, remove access to unmedicated bypass water during administration, and verify that medicated water reaches the full line. Resume ordinary water only after the intended volume is consumed.

The FMS entry should retain the individual trap series, exposure intervals, treatment calculation, product, each administration date, and second-dose due date. Keep post-treatment traps in the same positions. Request an earlier return if counts rise rapidly, affected locations multiply, or birds develop increasing irritation, feather disturbance, pallor, or reduced condition.
